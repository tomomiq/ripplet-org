I got back to Paris mid-week after a stopover in Toulouse to catch up with a friend. I'm glad to be home, and the thought of a few quiet weeks here, on the wings of what was a stimulating stay in the Ariège, makes me happy. Between the near-daily cycling expeditions and small-town socializing, there had been several "firsts" that I said yes to when the opportunity arose. I've brought some of that momentum home: I've ordered a few (more) books about the region, written down recipes I want to try, and already did some cycling and yoga to continue the athletic kick.
