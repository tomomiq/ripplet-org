Highlights: 

* I meandered along the Seine, from the Eiffel Tower to the Louvre, and got to see the beloved **Paris Olympic Cauldron** just as it was rising above the Tuileries. It was a magical moment, esp since it's often grounded by weather. 
* A **new designer** joined us at [AQ](https://aqworks.com/) 's Tokyo office. Someone joining our small, tight-knight team is a big deal, so we are excited! 
* I spent an afternoon planning a 2hr workshop called "**Enoughness and Money**" as part of a team-building arc with new collaborators. It was energizing to get creative about bringing this topic to the group, building on 5 years of work on money practices at Greaterthan. 
* On the weekend, I flew out to **Athens**. On Friday I had a fab dinner with a fab friend and on Saturday, joined a morning **stand-up paddle** session before our training module started.
