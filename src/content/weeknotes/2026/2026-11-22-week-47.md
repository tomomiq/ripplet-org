On Tuesday I co-hosted a **somatic resonance** workshop for coaching and org dev peers, then spent the weekend deep in the **somatic awareness** work that I started [three weeks ago](https://www.ripplet.org/weeknotes/2026/11/1/week-44-2026). 

As part of my Gestalt practice, my ingrained patterns of self-monitoring, inhibition, and control have been loosening. Somatic modalities are accelerating this shift, and on the other side of less self-interference, I'm discovering a growing sense of ease, connection, and aliveness in myself. 

I first encountered breathwork, meditation, and movement practices about five years, at the intersection of personal growth and professional development. I understood their potential benefits intellectually and was open to exploration, but was skeptical if it were for me. The penny dropped *today* that my preoccupation with whether something was "working for me" was getting in the way of experiencing it fully i.e. developing an embodied understanding of what it was really about—which, ironically, is precisely how these things work! 

Seeing how much I now enjoy these practices for their own sake is a welcome reminder that what feels inaccessible or even irrelevant today doesn't define what's within reach tomorrow. I'll gladly continue learning to get out of my own way 😆
