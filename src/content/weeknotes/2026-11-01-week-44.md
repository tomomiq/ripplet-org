The somatic dimension of coaching and personal development has been calling me for a while. In a conversation with a coach friend and mentor, I had a flashbulb moment when they declared the body as "the final frontier and the last mile" of development. 

It activated me enough to sign up for a three-day workshop on **somatic awareness** that would start the next day: a spontaneous decision, acting on what my body was telling me. 

Ironically, I’d misread the program description, and the workshop turned out to involve far more hours than I’d initially thought. Had I realized that beforehand, I'm not sure that I would have signed up on the spot. And since it turned out to be an incredible experience, I'm glad I didn't give myself the chance to overthink it! 

> And the day came when the risk to remain tight in a bud was more painful than the risk it took to blossom. -- Anaïs Nin
