It was *obon* week -- i.e. my summer break i.e. time for another cycling trip. This year, Estonia! 

A friend and I cycled 270km from **Tallinn** to **Sillamäe**, exploring **Lahemaa National Park** and following EuroVelo 10 Baltic Sea Cycle Route till the border with Russia. The coastline of the **Gulf of Finland** is completely flat, mostly low-traffic asphalt through Scots pine forest and a bit of gravel country road: very easy, enjoyable riding, with plenty of fun stops along the way--bogs, old-time manors, very cute fishing villages. 

Off the bike, I was glued to two [Sofi Oksanen](https://sofioksanen.com/) novels set under Soviet and German occupation. That, along with the excellent [folklore sci-fi](https://groveatlantic.com/book/the-man-who-spoke-snakish/) and Tove Jansson's [The Summer Book](https://tovejansson.com/book/summer-book/) that I'd read in the lead-up, gave me another way into a region I'd had almost no prior exposure to. 

We ate well, rested well, and had no mechanicals or injuries. The weather was sunny, with a few cloudy afternoons, with temperatures around 20 degrees which felt so good after [Italy](https://www.ripplet.org/cycling-western-alps) same time last year. 

We'd flown in/out of **Helsinki**, crossing to Tallinn by ferry (an experience in itself), so  I even got to visit the  **Moomin Museum** in **Tampere** before heading home. 

I'm grateful for a relaxed summer, with great health, friendships and exploration.
