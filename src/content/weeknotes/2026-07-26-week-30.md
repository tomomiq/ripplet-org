It's not August yet but summer is in the air, and I don't mean just the weather or the forest fires that are ravaging many locations in France. 

With certain friends we've replaced restaurants for long walks as much as possible. It makes for a great combination of meandering conversations, people watching and environment watching. Last week I went to **Bois de Boulogne**, where the lower shrubbery are burnt away, and this week I walked up and down **Canal Saint Martin**, where people were swimming even though we were between heat waves. 

The same feeling carried over into big events. I went to Christopher Nolan's "**The Odyssey**" at the Grand Rex (70mm film!), the final stage of the **Tour de France** in Montmartre, and an open-air showing of **Princess Mononoke** at [Parc de la Villette](https://www.lavillette.com/manifestations/cinema-en-plein-air-26/). You see these long queues and worry about overflow but somehow it works out. It's like the city just absorbs them.

I went through a wide range of books, **choosing reading instead of exercise** every single day--a dilemma that I haven't been able to sort out yet. I would never regret reading but there is some chagrin, because there's another heat wave coming next week.

But what wonderfully eclectic reading! It's starting to dawn on me that **the more I read, the more there is to read**. Books points me towards other books. Talking about books elicits recommendations. I follow more booktubers and blogs, which means more recommendations. And whenever I enjoy an author, I gleefully pick up another of their books.
